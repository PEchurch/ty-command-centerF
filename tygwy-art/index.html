About & speaking

Ty "GWY" Wilson writes from Tahlequah, Oklahoma, in the Cherokee Nation. His work draws on family, on what elders told him, and on the documented record, and it is written for Cherokee and Black Cherokee families first.

Available for readings, school visits, panels and talks on Cherokee language, Cherokee Freedmen history and storytelling.
