About Ty

Author, artist and storyteller from Tahlequah, Oklahoma, in the Cherokee Nation.

Biography

Ty "GWY" Wilson writes and makes art from Tahlequah, Oklahoma, in the Cherokee Nation. His work draws on family, on what elders told him, and on the documented record, and it is written for Cherokee and Black Cherokee families first.

He is the author of the Cherokee language children's books 1st American: Cherokee and Forest Relatives, and the creator of the United Native Nations Universe (UNNU), an alternate history told through novels and film.

Ty is available for readings, school visits, panels and talks on Cherokee language, Cherokee Freedmen history and storytelling.
