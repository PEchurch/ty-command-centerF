## Premise (draft logline)
Napoleon Hill gave readers the plan and Paramahansa Yogananda gave them the purpose. Minister Ty GWY Wilson's Positive Energy way of life joins them: set a definite goal, test it against service and conscience, and pursue it through daily organized labor, with money treated as fuel rather than the destination.

## Core argument
1. A disciplined mind shapes the life you live (Hill and Yogananda agree).
2. Purpose comes first, then the plan: Yogananda's test (is it right for me, does it serve others?), then Hill's specifics (what, by when, in exchange for what).
3. Prosperity is internal alignment, gratitude, and openness; wealth is external fuel. "There is enough, and I am enough."
4. Positive energy is practiced, not just felt. It shows up as action.
5. Success is measured by impact.

## The book's signature move
Ty's own life is the case study: a written, present-tense purpose statement, a business plan with deadlines, and a hub-and-spoke set of ventures that all feed one mission. The reader watches the method used by the person teaching it, including the tension between a billion-dollar goal and "prosperity is not possession."

## Reader **[CONFIRM]**
