# Positive Energy Self-Help Book: Outline (v1, for approval)

Each chapter: a story, a teaching, a Hill/Yogananda bridge, a PE practice, and a short action step. Target: ~3,500–5,000 words per chapter (about 45–60k words total), adjustable once length is confirmed.

**Chapter template**
1. Opening story (Ty's life, a community moment, or a short historical scene)
2. The teaching (plain language)
3. Hill says / Yogananda says / Positive Energy says
4. Practice (do-this-today)
5. Reflection questions
6. One-line takeaway

---

## Part One: The Foundation

**1. The Power You Were Born With**
Control of your own mind. Thoughts as habits and magnets. Hill's "whatever the mind can conceive and believe" meets Yogananda's thought-and-light. PE: positive energy is practiced, not just felt. *Practice:* Three-Breath Reset.

**2. Your Definite Purpose**
Desire made specific. Hill's five parts (what, by when, what you give, plan, written) plus Yogananda's test (right for you, serves others). Ty's present-tense purpose statement as the live example. *Practice:* write your Daily Intention Statement.

**3. Faith and Belief, Without Fear**
Building faith on purpose. Hill's six ghosts of fear; Yogananda on the eternal soul dissolving fear. Honest note on sources and what the evidence does and doesn't support. *Practice:* name your fear, write what it protects, answer it.

## Part Two: The Inner Life

**4. Words, Thoughts, and Affirmation**
Autosuggestion and the spoken word. Read your goal aloud twice a day with feeling. How to avoid empty positivity: affirmation tied to action. *Practice:* morning and evening affirmation.

**5. Stillness, Meditation, and Intuition**
Hill's sixth sense and Yogananda's intuition. Answers surface when a problem is allowed to rest. Sacred rest. *Practice:* Morning Grounding Practice (4-2-6 breath) plus ten minutes of silence.

**6. Gratitude as the Bridge**
Hill's gratitude prayer: ask for wisdom to govern your own mind, not for more. Gratitude as the meeting point of plan and spirit. *Practice:* daily gratitude ledger.

## Part Three: The Outer Work

**7. Organized Daily Labor**
Rituals and schedules: meals, exercise, meditation, and daily action on the micro and macro visions. Why the flow, once found, stops feeling like work. *Practice:* build your daily schedule.

**8. The Master Mind: Building in Harmony**
Peers who sharpen you plus one teacher further down the road. Ty's hub-and-spoke model: one mission, many ventures helping each other. Weekly Master Mind meeting format. *Practice:* form or join a circle.

**9. Specialized Knowledge and Imagination**
Borrow expertise, recombine ideas, ask for new ones. Create once, use everywhere (one shoot becomes a film, a video, radio, posts, merch, and a chapter). *Practice:* map your own content or skill pipeline.

## Part Four: Prosperity

**10. Money Is Fuel, Not the Destination**
The central tension: a billion-dollar goal and "prosperity is not possession." Hill's definite number as focus; Yogananda's test as guardrail; PE's rule that impact is the measure. Keeping church and business money separate, with professional advice. *Practice:* write your "what the money makes possible" statement.

**11. Going the Extra Mile: Service as Strategy**
Hill's quality and quantity of service plus mental attitude, Yogananda's service-based success, PE's Service pillar. Examples from community outreach. *Practice:* one unasked act of service per day for a week.

## Part Five: Staying the Course

**12. Persistence Through Hard Seasons**
Hill's persistence and the seed of an equivalent benefit. Yogananda's karma as training. Resilience. Rest and delegation. *Practice:* a hard-season plan.

**13. Leaving Every Place Better**
Legacy. The Ten Principles in action. The life's work "by any means necessary" read through kindness and integrity. *Practice:* your legacy sentence and the next ninety days.
