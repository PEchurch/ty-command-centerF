# Ty "GWY" Wilson: Author Bio

Master bio for all books and websites, in four lengths.

## Long bio

**Ty "GWY" Wilson** is a Cherokee historian, author, filmmaker, artist, cultural preservationist and minister from Tahlequah, Oklahoma. He grew up surrounded by Cherokee culture, oral history, traditions and music, and he has made it his life's work to preserve history in its entirety, including the stories that mainstream narratives have too often left out.

He is the founder and president of the Cherokees for Black Indian History Preservation Foundation. He serves on the Cherokee Principal Chief's Advisory Committee on Freedmen Art & History and on the board of the Oklahoma Blues Hall of Fame.

Wilson is the author of *Cherokee Freedmen: We Are Cherokee*; *The Ledger and the Land: Slavery, Wealth, and the Black Cherokee Economy Before and After Removal*; the novel *The Road to Mexico: A Novel of the Cherokee Slave Revolt of 1842*; and the children's books *Forest Relatives* and the *1st American* series (*1st American: Cherokee*, *1st American: Choctaw* and *1st American: Keetoowah*). He is also co-author and coeditor of *Oklahoma Black Cherokees*. His writing combines historical research, oral tradition, legal history, economics, genealogy and personal storytelling. It is deeply researched and written for general readers.

He is also the creator of the **United Native Nations Universe (UNNU)**, an alternate-history book series set in a world where Native nations became the dominant continental power after European conquest collapsed in the Americas. Drawing inspiration from the history of Native nations, Black Indigenous communities and the cultural traditions of Oklahoma, the series blends history, culture, music, spirituality, law and political drama to create a fictional world that asks difficult questions without offering easy answers. It asks how survival, power, identity and justice shape nations across generations, and its questions are for any reader.

The Universe begins with the digital graphic novel ***United Native Nations: Origins – The Honey Springs Testimony***, now available, a fictional reconstruction of the Battle of Honey Springs. It is followed by the first novel, ***The Weight of Survival***, which begins in 2026. In a world where Native nations have secured their sovereignty, political unrest, buried historical testimony and social inequality threaten the carefully maintained national story of the United Native Nations, and the nation must confront the difficult choices that come with governing a powerful nation. Together they open an expanding Universe of novels and graphic stories about the human cost of power, the responsibility of memory and the meaning of freedom.

Wilson's storytelling is grounded in the belief that history is more than a record of what happened. It is also a reflection of who was allowed to tell the story, whose voices were preserved and whose experiences were forgotten.

Beyond writing, Wilson works in film, photography, jewelry making, songwriting, poetry and visual art. His mission is the same in every medium: to preserve history honestly, completely and for future generations.

The "GWY" in his name comes from the Cherokee syllabary and the word "Cherokee." It reflects his commitment to telling the story from within the community, from the perspective of the people who lived it.

Wilson lives in Oklahoma, where he continues to write, research, lecture and advocate for the preservation of history and cultural memory.
