## 5. Cross-promotion mechanics
1. Every Friday release is featured in the next Saturday weekly video (website, 1:00 PM CT recommended); there is no live broadcast and no Sunday repeat. After PE Frequency radio launches, add radio rotation (weekly-video.md section 9).
2. Each release gets a "release kit": 3 clips, 1 quote card, 1 email blurb, 1 weekly-video segment, 1 merch tie-in, 1 Beat reflection.
3. Service clips feed the next release's teaser; releases feed the service closing song.
4. A release-day email goes to the single list segmented by interest (music, books, merch).
5. Shoutout rotation: each release names the venture that follows it.
6. Monthly metrics: pre-saves, streams, orders, email sign-ups, donations per release.
