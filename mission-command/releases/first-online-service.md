## 6. Accessibility
- Live captions: choose one: platform auto-captions (free but error-prone; review), a human CART service, or a volunteer typing into a caption encoder. Cost/vendor not verified. Always run a test on Dec 12 and Jan 9.
- Replay: edited caption file (SRT) uploaded within 48 hours.
- Audio quality over visual flourish; clear speech pace; describe on-screen graphics aloud.
- High-contrast lower thirds, large type; a text transcript posted with the replay.
- Provide a phone-in or email prayer/support option for people with limited bandwidth (optional).

## 7. Moderation
- 2 moderators plus 1 backup, with a pinned code of conduct (kind, respectful, no solicitation) rooted in the PE Pillars.

## 9. Flags for attorney/CPA (do not resolve without them)
- Music licensing and public performance: live-streamed and archived songs (original, cover, licensed tracks). Church-stream licenses such as CCLI's streaming add-on exist, but sources I found were dated and disagree on whether platforms cover music (see sources). Ask an attorney which licenses apply, whether PRO licenses are needed, and whether archived replays are covered. Default to original/cleared music until answered.
- Giving: church donation language, receipts, and separation of church and business money; a CPA reviews.
- Recording consent for guests, musicians, and any visible viewers; images and releases.
- Child safety: no children on camera without written guardian consent.
