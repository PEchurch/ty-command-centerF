## 6. Accessibility
- Option B (recommended): captions are a human-corrected file attached before the premiere. Auto-captions alone are not enough for recorded video, per university accessibility guidance ([Berkeley DAP](https://dap.berkeley.edu/learn/techniques/live-captioning-faq); not a legal standard).
- Option A: live captions via platform auto-captions (free but error-prone), a caption encoder with a volunteer typist, or a human CART service. Cost and vendor not verified. Run a test on Dec 12 and Jan 9. A corrected caption file replaces the live captions on the replay within 48 hours.
- Post a transcript with the replay.
- Audio quality over visual flourish; clear speech pace; describe on-screen graphics aloud.
- High-contrast lower thirds, large type.
- Provide an email prayer/support option for people with limited bandwidth (optional).

## 9. Flags for attorney/CPA (do not resolve without them)
- Music licensing and public performance: streamed and archived songs (original, cover, licensed tracks). Church-stream licenses such as CCLI's streaming add-on exist, but sources I found were dated and disagree on whether platforms cover music (see sources). Ask an attorney which licenses apply, whether PRO licenses are needed, and whether archived replays are covered. Default to original/cleared music until answered.
- Giving: church donation language, receipts, and separation of church and business money; a CPA reviews. Donation processor is not yet chosen (plan.md).
- Recording consent for guests, musicians, and any visible viewers; images and releases.
- Child safety: no children on camera without written guardian consent.
