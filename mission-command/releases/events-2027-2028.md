### Event format (about 2 hours)
Welcome (10) / Set 1 (30) / Spotlight story or elder feature (10) / Set 2 (35) / Giving or service moment (10) / Finale (20) / Close (5).
Produce as pre-recorded-live hybrid where possible: live host with recorded performances protects against technical failure and eases rights (rights still need an attorney).

### Lineup needs (per event)
- 1 headliner (PE Frequency artist or guest), 2 to 3 supporting acts, 1 host, 1 house band or backing tracks, 1 youth/community act.
- Booking: written agreements covering performance, recording, rebroadcast, and 90-day to perpetual replay rights; fee for each act [CONFIRM budget]. Attorney reviews the template.
- Pipeline: shortlist 12 acts by Jan 2027; confirm headliner 12 weeks out; supporting acts 8 weeks out.

### Ticketing or giving model (options; legal/tax flags)
| Model | Pros | Cons / flags |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Free with donation ask | Max reach, mission-aligned | Giving must be accurate and CPA-reviewed |
| Pay-what-you-can / suggested donation | Balanced | Tax receipt wording (quid pro quo) is a CPA/attorney question |
| Fixed ticket + replay | Predictable income | Ticket income might be taxable (UBIT) for a church or nonprofit: CPA; sales tax; platform fees: unverified |
| Hybrid: free live, paid replay + merch bundle | Feeds BLK-FTHR and 1st N8TIONS | Flash-sale proceeds to the Arts Fund must be accounted for separately; CPA |
Recommendation (pending CPA): free live with a suggested donation and a "Support the mission" link; merch/book bundles through the store. Keep church and business money separate (plan.md). Ticketing/legal questions go to an attorney or CPA; do not launch without them.

### Sponsorship
- Tiers: presenting, mission partner, community partner, in-kind (equipment, food, hotel).
- Targets: Native-owned businesses, local arts councils, heritage organizations, and faith/community foundations (names not verified; research needed).
- Terms: sponsor logos on the broadcast/events; any benefits that resemble business advertising can create tax questions for a church/nonprofit (UBIT): CPA review. Contracts by attorney. Keep sponsor money in the correct entity.

### Safety
Written safety plan (crowd management, emergency exits, medical, weather/lightning, fire, security, lost-child, communication tree), a licensed professional where required, local fire marshal sign-off, insurance (general liability, possibly liquor/event cancellation; unverified), rehearsal of evacuation, volunteer briefings, and an incident log. Local law and insurer determine specifics.

### Accessibility
Wheelchair routes and seating, accessible restrooms, ASL interpretation (on stage and picture-in-picture on the stream), live captions, quiet space, large print programs, accessible parking and drop-off, clear signage, service-animal policy. Plan accessibility from venue selection (ADA compliance: attorney/consultant).

### Streaming production
Multi-camera crew with switcher, redundant encoders and bonded/dual internet (or cellular bonding), audio feed from front-of-house, a director, graphic operator, captioner, chat moderators, and a remote host. Rehearse the stream with the full lineup. Record ISO tracks. Decide on platform (decide in 2027 for the first service) and a plan B.
