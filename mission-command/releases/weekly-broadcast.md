## 4. Content sourcing (create once, use everywhere)
- Music: PE Frequency originals first (rights clean); licensed or third-party music only after rights check (attorney flag). Keep a rights log: song, owner, license, expiry, proof.
- Interviews: elder interviews already built into Ty's 1:30 PM mission/service block; capture with a release form; one interview feeds one show segment, 2 to 3 clips, a Beat post, and possibly a book/documentary asset.
- Service clips: each third-Saturday service yields 3 to 5 clips used in the next three broadcasts.
- Film/doc/music video (due before Feb 1, 2027): behind-the-scenes cuts feed the interview and release-spotlight segments.
- Pipeline folder (proposed): /content/{music, interviews, service-clips, wisdom, promos}, each with a "cleared" subfolder. Production-producer owns the rights tracker; this plan reads from it.
