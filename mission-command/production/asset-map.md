# Asset Map: One Shoot, Many Assets (DRAFT)

Principle (plan.md): create once, use everywhere. Shoot weekend Nov 14-15, 2026 (backup Nov 21-22) feeds: documentary short, music video, PE Frequency radio, The Beat posts, merch design, and a book chapter. Every shot must have a signed release and location permission (rights-tracker.md).

## Capture standards
- Primary camera: 4K or 1080p, 24 fps, shoot 4K and protect 9:16 crops (frame the center 40% safe for vertical).
- Audio: lav + shotgun on all interviews; record 60 sec room tone at each location; separate clean performance audio from playback.
- Stills: shoot RAW stills on every setup (merch, book, posters).
- Back up in 3 places before leaving location; label cards by day/camera.
- Slate every take; record names and spellings on camera.
- Ask each person on camera two bonus lines: "say your name and how you want to be credited" and "one sentence of blessing/encouragement" (social + radio).

## Rules for reuse
1. Merch and book use needs explicit scope in the release (R1 in rights-tracker.md).
2. No sacred or ceremonial imagery on merch; advisor reviews all art.
3. Each channel owner receives selects, not raw files; originals stay in the archive.
4. File naming: YYYYMMDD_Project_Camera_Reel_Clip. Folder tree: /01_Footage /02_Audio /03_Stills /04_Edit /05_Delivery /06_Rights.
5. Rough-cut teasers wait until releases and music are cleared.
