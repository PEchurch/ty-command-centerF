## Delivery specs (proposed defaults; confirm with each festival/platform at submission)
- Master: ProRes 422 HQ (or 422), 1920x1080, 23.976 or 24 fps (match source), Rec.709, stereo 48 kHz 24-bit WAV.
- Web/online service: H.264/H.265 MP4, 1080p, about 10-16 Mbps, AAC stereo, burned-in nothing; separate captions.
- Captions: English SRT/VTT for every piece (accessibility); subtitles in Cherokee only with advisor review.
- Festival screener: H.264 1080p with private link; DCP only if selected (about $300-$800 service; unverified).
- Extras: poster, 3-5 stills, trailer or 30 sec cut, bio/synopsis, credit list, music cue sheet, E&O/clearance log if requested.
- Social cuts (see asset-map.md): 9:16, 1:1, 16:9.
