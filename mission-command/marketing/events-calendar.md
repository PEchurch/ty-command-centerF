## How to read this
Verification status key:
- **SECONDARY-SOURCE**: a date or fee appeared in a third-party listing in search results; the organizer's own page was not confirmed. Treat as tentative.
- **PATTERN ONLY**: no 2027 date was found; the date is inferred from past years. Do NOT book around it.
- **CALCULATED**: a calendar fact I computed (e.g., June 19, 2027 is a Saturday), not a sourced event listing.
- **CONFLICT**: sources disagree.
- **UNVERIFIED**: nothing found.

Limits of this pass: my page-fetch tool could not reach organizer sites (visitcherokeenation.com, redearth.org), so I relied on search-result summaries. Every item needs one confirmation pass on the organizer's own site or by phone before any commitment. Most 2027 schedules are not yet published (as of Oct 25, 2026).

Cultural care: attending, vending, or filming at tribal events requires respect for each host's rules. Many Native markets are restricted to enrolled citizens or Indigenous artists; Ty's eligibility is not in plan.md [CONFIRM]. Ask permission before photographing or filming dancers, ceremonies, or regalia, and follow posted no-photo rules. Do not sell at a Native-only market unless eligible.
