Until resolved, wording must be a defined dollar or percent amount, never vague "proceeds."
  4. Donation receipts and year-end acknowledgments (timing, required language, quid pro quo for any thank-you gifts), and whether any merch premiums are part-gift, part-purchase.
  5. UBIT and mission-locked subsidiary questions already in plan.md.
- **Cultural care.** Native American Heritage Month is about Native peoples' living cultures, not a sales hook. Required before launch:
  - Ty confirms his own tribal affiliation and how he wants to speak about it [CONFIRM]. Do not state an affiliation in copy that Ty has not approved.
  - Any story about a specific Native person, family, nation, ceremony, or design motif needs that person's or nation's consent. Do not use sacred or ceremonial imagery, regalia photos, or tribal names/symbols as decoration or merch themes without permission.
  - Credit and, where appropriate, compensate Native artists, speakers, and musicians featured. Prefer Native-owned voices over speaking about them.
  - Do not describe any Nation's history, language, or protocol from memory; cite or have a community member review it.
  - Avoid "mascot" style imagery, "Indian" stereotypes, and "spirit animal"-type language.
- **Rights.** Confirm licenses for all music, footage, and images (plan.md rule) before reuse.
- **Dependency.** Websites finalize before Nov 1 (web-launch-lead). Donation page, email capture, and Shopify flash-sale tooling must be tested before Week 1 asks. If the donation page is not live, hold all asks and run story-only content.
