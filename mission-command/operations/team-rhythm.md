## 1. Weekly shape
| Day | Team focus | Meetings | Weekly video step |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Monday | Plan; goals and deadlines | Master Mind (45 min) | PLAN: lock this week's episode sheet and the next two; check music is on the rights log (Ty + producer, about 15 min of Ty) |
| Tuesday | Creative: film, books, design (maker time) | None | RECORD: Ty records the wisdom segment; camera/audio person on set. Batch two episodes every other week |
| Wednesday | Business and marketing: content, store, email, partners | One optional 20-min stand-up | EDIT: editor builds the rough cut from cleared folders; guest release in hand |
| Thursday | Church and The Beat: service prep, music, community | Optional 20-min prep session | CAPTIONS: final cut; auto-captions corrected by a human; Ty checks Cherokee words and names |
| Friday | Admin and money; close open loops | Weekly review (30 min, below) | UPLOAD: captions attached, title and thumbnail, upload, schedule; clips and email blurb queued. Ty approves by 3:00 PM CT |
| Saturday | Rest by default. Producer only: confirm the video went live and the caption track works by 1:15 PM CT | None | AUTO-PUBLISH (recommended 1:00 PM CT) |
| Sunday | Rest. Pre-scheduled share only | None; no requests expected | None |

Each person's load: Ty about 2 to 2.5 hours a week (details in daily-schedule.md). Producer about 3 to 4 hours, editor about 4 to 6 hours, caption lead about 1 to 2 hours (estimates, to be checked after the first two episodes). These are estimates, not measured.

### How the video avoids overloading Ty
1. Ty does only what only Ty can: the wisdom segment, pronunciation and cultural checks, and the Friday approval.
2. Plan and upload belong to the producer. Ty's Monday and Friday parts are a review, not production.
3. Bank two episodes by December; never publish the week you record. Batch two recordings on one Tuesday every other week so the other week's Tuesday stays creative.
4. Hard cutoff: if not captioned and approved by Fri 3:00 PM CT, the evergreen backup episode publishes. Nobody works Friday night or Saturday to save a deadline.
5. Holiday weeks (Dec 26, Jan 2) run best-of or banked videos.
6. Lean pilot: first video 10 to 12 minutes (Nov 7, or Nov 14 if capacity is short).
7. If the producer or editor is not named by Oct 30, start Nov 14 instead of Nov 7, and shorten the format rather than ask Ty to produce it.
8. If Ty's energy is 1-2 two weeks in a row, publish the backup and skip a recording week.

## 3. Meeting limits
- Team total: Master Mind (45 min) plus Friday review (30 min) plus max two 20-min working sessions per week.
- No meeting without agenda, owner, and desired outcome; cancel if none.
- No meetings Tuesday (creative day) or before 10:30 AM. Recording is not a meeting; it is scheduled maker time.
- Anyone can decline a meeting with no agenda.

## 5. Handoff rules
See workflows.md section 3. Short form: one message, one owner, one due date, rights status, file link; acknowledge within one business day; nothing publishes without logged approval.

## 6. Weekly review (Friday, 30 min)
1. What shipped (including this week's video scheduled) and what slipped, and why.
2. Open handoffs older than 5 business days.
3. Approvals waiting on Ty.
4. Next week's deadlines (from plan.md); are two episodes banked ahead?
5. Energy check: 1-5 for each person; anyone at 1-2 gets help, not more.
6. One thing to stop, delegate, or automate.

## 7. Rest rules
- Saturday and Sunday: no assigned work except the producer's 15-minute Saturday publish check. Sunday has none.
- After a major release or deadline (Nov 1 sites, Jan 16 service, Feb 1 film items), schedule a recovery half day within 3 days. [CONFIRM]
- Team uses Ty's "worn out" ritual as permission: rest is part of the work. Three-Breath Reset: release, welcome, commit.
- Any person with more than 2 consecutive weeks at energy 1-2 triggers a workload conversation.
- Shoot weekends (Nov 14-15, 2026): the weekly video is pre-scheduled, so the crew is free to shoot; Sunday rest still holds for Ty.
