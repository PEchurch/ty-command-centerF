# Team Rhythm and Rest/Delegation (DRAFT)

Goal (Ty's words): once the flow is right, the work does not feel like work; the team enjoys the pursuit of the mission. Nothing here is sent to the team or put on shared calendars until Ty approves.

## 1. Weekly shape
| Day | Team focus | Meetings |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Monday | Plan; goals and deadlines | Master Mind (45 min) |
| Tuesday | Creative: film, books, design (maker time) | None |
| Wednesday | Business and marketing: content, store, email, partners | One optional 20-min stand-up |
| Thursday | Church and The Beat: service prep, music, community | Optional 20-min service/broadcast prep |
| Friday | Admin and money; close open loops | Weekly review (30 min, below) |
| Saturday | Positive Energy broadcast 1-2 PM; 3rd Saturday live service | Broadcast crew only |
| Sunday | Rest; broadcast repeat; light look-ahead | None; no requests expected |

## 2. Daily rhythm (team)
| Window | Purpose |
| --- | --- |
| Morning (until about 10:30) | Focus: no meetings, no chat expected. Matches Ty's deep creative block |
| Late morning | Business work and replies |
| Early afternoon | Collaboration: calls, community, service work |
| After 3:30 PM | Admin, follow-up, handoffs |
| Evening and Sunday | Rest. Urgent items only by agreed channel |

## 3. Meeting limits
- Team total: Master Mind (45 min) plus Friday review (30 min) plus max two 20-min working sessions per week.
- No meeting without agenda, owner, and desired outcome; cancel if none.
- No meetings Tuesday (creative day) or before 10:30 AM.
- Anyone can decline a meeting with no agenda.

## 4. Response times (proposed) [CONFIRM]
| Type | Target |
| --- | --- |
| Urgent (site down, order failure, live broadcast problem) | Same day, within 2 hours in working hours |
| Handoff acknowledgment | 1 business day |
| Approval request, Tier 2 | 48 hours |
| General question | 2 business days |
| Outside working hours / Sunday | Not expected unless broadcast issue |

Channels: one for urgent (phone or text), one for everything else (shared board or email). Do not use urgent for non-urgent.

## 5. Handoff rules
See workflows.md section 3. Short form: one message, one owner, one due date, rights status, file link; acknowledge within one business day; nothing publishes without logged approval.

## 6. Weekly review (Friday, 30 min)
1. What shipped; what slipped, and why.
2. Open handoffs older than 5 business days.
3. Approvals waiting on Ty.
4. Next week's deadlines (from plan.md).
5. Energy check: 1-5 for each person; anyone at 1-2 gets help, not more.
6. One thing to stop, delegate, or automate.

## 7. Rest rules
- Sunday is rest. No assigned work Sunday except the broadcast repeat.
- After a major release or deadline (Nov 1 sites, Jan 16 service, Feb 1 film items), schedule a recovery half day within 3 days. [CONFIRM]
- Team uses Ty's "worn out" ritual as permission: rest is part of the work. Three-Breath Reset: release, welcome, commit.
- Any person with more than 2 consecutive weeks at energy 1-2 triggers a workload conversation.

## 8. Rest and delegation recommendations

### Where the plan is overloaded (flagged, not crammed)
1. **Nov-Dec 2026 collision:** websites by Nov 1, giving season campaign (Nov, GivingTuesday Dec 1, year-end), three new-store holiday rushes, and weekly broadcasts all land at once. Ty cannot create, approve, and publish all of this alone.
2. **Dec 2026 - Feb 1, 2027:** first online service Jan 16, doc short plus music video before Feb 1, BLK-FTHR film, and book printing all overlap. The doc short and music video need shoot, edit, rights, and festival planning in about 14 weeks.
3. **Ty's day:** morning stack takes about 2.5 hours of rituals; deep creative block is 2.5 hours; business block is six jobs in 1.75 hours. Only Tuesday is truly creative-weighted.
4. **Eleven goals with deadlines in plan.md** versus "one action toward each daily": needs a ledger (see daily-schedule.md).
5. **Weekend load:** Saturday broadcast plus Sunday repeat leaves no full day off if prep slips.
6. **Eight ventures with one decision-maker:** Ty is the approval bottleneck unless approval tiers are adopted.

Recommendation: do not add anything new in Nov-Dec without removing something. Ask the chief of staff to sequence goals so no more than two major deliverables are active in any month. [CONFIRM]

### Delegate
| Task | Why | To (role) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Order handling, customer replies, shipping tracking | Not creative; recurring | Operations/fulfillment assistant |
| Social scheduling and weekly Beat posts (Tier 1) | Template-driven | Social/community manager |
| Email sends and list hygiene | Recurring | Email/marketing assistant |
| Video editing and repurposing clips | Skilled time sink | Editor (freelance or volunteer) |
| Bookkeeping and invoices | Friday work; needs accuracy | Bookkeeper; CPA review |
| Broadcast logistics (audio check, upload, repeat) | Fixed weekly | Broadcast producer |
| Rights and licensing tracking | Detail work | Content coordinator, attorney for sign-off |
| Event calendar research | One-time then maintenance | campaign-planner |

### Automate
- Weekly content scheduling (batch Wednesday, publish by scheduler).
- Order confirmation, shipping, and thank-you emails.
- Monthly metrics pull into one sheet (see metrics.md).
- Meeting pre-form reminders and action tracker reminders.
- Broadcast repeat on Sundays (pre-loaded).
- Donation receipts (consult CPA on wording).

### Keep for Ty only
Deep creative work (BLK-FTHR, books, music), the sermon/message, doctrine and history claims, community and elder relationships, final approvals for Tier 2 and 3, and the morning rituals.

### Ask for help
Ty's hard-day ritual: "find someone who needs help and give it" also works in reverse: name the three tasks that would free the most time and hand them off this week.
