## 3. Hand-off rules (cross-venture)
1. A handoff is one message with: what, who receives, due date, rights status, where the files are. No handoff by memory.
2. Receiver acknowledges within 1 business day; says "accepted" or "need X."
3. Nothing publishes without a logged approval and a rights line.
4. Orders flow: store order -> fulfillment owner -> shipped -> tracking sent. Weekly review of anything older than 5 business days.
5. Money: each sale and donation is recorded under its own entity (church vs business). Flash-sale proceeds for 1st N8TIONS are tagged for the Arts Fund. CPA and attorney review is required before structure is final (UBIT, mission-locked subsidiary, trust). Draft only; this document gives no legal or tax advice.

## 4. Key handoff flows
- **Hub -> spokes (weekly):** broadcast and service content -> PE Frequency, Beat posts, email list.
- **Art/film -> shops:** Art or film still approved -> BLK-FTHR print -> CLUB 1907 / PE Clothing / 1st N8TIONS listing -> store -> email announcement -> Beat post.
