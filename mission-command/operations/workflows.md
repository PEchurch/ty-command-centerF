## 3. Hand-off rules (cross-venture)
1. A handoff is one message with: what, who receives, due date, rights status, owning entity of the sender and receiver, where the files are. No handoff by memory.
2. Receiver acknowledges within 1 business day; says "accepted" or "need X."
3. Nothing publishes without a logged approval and a rights line.
4. Orders flow: store order -> fulfillment owner -> shipped -> tracking sent. Weekly review of anything older than 5 business days.
5. Money: each sale and donation is recorded under its own entity (church vs Ty-and-Lee business). Flash-sale proceeds for 1st N8TIONS are tagged for the Arts Fund. CPA and attorney review is required before structure is final (UBIT, mission-locked subsidiary, trust). Draft only.
6. A handoff that crosses the church/private line follows section 6.

Process for any handoff on this list:
1. Sender names the owning entity on both sides in the handoff message.
2. Item is tagged "boundary" on the intake board and treated as Tier 3.
3. Bookkeeper keeps it in a "boundary log" (date, item, entities, amount, document link).
4. The log is reviewed at the monthly metrics meeting and sent to the CPA and attorney on a schedule they set.
5. Nothing above is final until the CPA and attorney have reviewed it.
