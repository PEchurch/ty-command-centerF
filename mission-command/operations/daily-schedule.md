## Rituals for hard days (Ty's)
- Stuck: find the "can do" part and start where you stand.
- Anger: laugh it off, then five minutes of action on the mission.
- Fear about money: say "There is enough, and I am enough," then do one task that brings in income today.
- Worn out: rest is part of the work. Three-Breath Reset: release, welcome, commit.
- Sorry for yourself: find someone who needs help and give it.
