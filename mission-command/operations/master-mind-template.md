# Weekly Master Mind Meeting (DRAFT)

Fits Ty's weekly rhythm: Monday is "plan the week, review goals and deadlines, Mastermind check-in with your team." Proposed slot: Monday, 45 minutes, time [CONFIRM]. Hard stop at 45 minutes. Not sent to anyone; no calendar changes made.

## Ground rules
- Start with one breath together and read the one-line mission aloud: "High Spirits. Deep Roots. Total Love."
- Each venture speaks 3 minutes max, in the same order, using the same four prompts.
- Decisions are made or assigned an owner and a date. No decision is left floating.
- Hard-day tone: if someone is worn out, "rest is part of the work." Offer help, not pressure.

## Agenda (45 minutes)
| Min | Item | Who |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0-3 | Opening: breath, mission line, win of the week | Ty |

| 30-38 | Decisions needed (max 3) | Ty |
| 38-43 | Action items read back; owner and date confirmed | Notes-taker |
| 43-45 | One-line mission check and close | Ty |

## Monthly add-on (first Monday of the month, +15 min)

- What can we stop, delegate, or automate?
