MANUSCRIPT → PRINT-READY PDF
IngramSpark-ready · handled by Agent SIGNAL (BLK-FTHR Media)
FULL PIPELINE
1
Manuscript Intake & Assessment

Import final manuscript (Word .docx or Google Doc). Establish trim size (IngramSpark standard: 6×9, 5.5×8.5, or 5×8). Confirm chapter count, front matter, back matter, index, bibliography. Set ISBN and copyright page info.

Word / Google DocsIngramSpark specsISBN
2
Interior Layout & Typesetting

Set margins (IngramSpark min: 0.5" outer, 0.75" inner/gutter for 300+ pages). Apply consistent heading hierarchy (H1 chapters, H2 sections). Body font 11–12pt with 1.3–1.5 leading. Page numbers in footer/header. Drop caps or ornamental chapter openers. Running heads with author/title.

Adobe InDesignAffinity PublisherVellum (Mac)Reedsy
3
Front & Back Matter

Front: Half title, full title page, copyright page (ISBN, edition, rights statement), dedication, foreword/preface, table of contents. Back: Bibliography (Chicago style for historical works), index, about the author, other titles by author, colophon.

Chicago 17th ed.Manual index or indexing software
4
Images / Photos (if applicable)

All images must be 300 DPI minimum. Embed as CMYK or grayscale (for B&W interior). Caption every image. If photos of historical documents: ensure contrast is maximized. Place images inline with text or in signature plates.

300 DPI minCMYK or GrayscalePhotoshop / GIMP
5
Preflight & PDF Export

Export as PDF/X-1a or PDF/X-4 (IngramSpark accepts both). Embed all fonts. Flatten transparency. No crop marks (IngramSpark adds bleed). Color profile: FOGRA39 (Europe) or SWOP (US). File size under 400MB. Check: widow/orphan lines, bad line breaks, hyphenation, page count divisible by 2.

PDF/X-1a or X-4Fonts embeddedAdobe Preflight
6
Cover File (separate)

Full wrap cover: front + spine + back as single flat PDF. Spine width = page count × paper thickness (IngramSpark calculator). Resolution: 300 DPI. Color: CMYK. Bleed: 0.125" all sides. Barcode placement on back (lower right, no bleed). IngramSpark provides template generator.

IngramSpark cover templateAdobe Illustrator / Canva Pro300 DPI CMYK
7
Upload & Proof

Upload interior PDF and cover PDF to IngramSpark. Order a physical proof copy before approving. Review: margins, spine text, bleed, image clarity, page count. Approve print-ready status. Set wholesale discount (40–55% recommended for retailer distribution).

IngramSpark portalPhysical proofDistribution settings

MANUSCRIPT → EBOOK
EPUB 3 · Kindle KFX · publish-ready for all major platforms
CONVERSION PIPELINE
1
Clean the Source Document

Start from the final edited manuscript (Word .docx). Remove all manual formatting: no hard spaces, no forced page breaks, no tab indents (use paragraph styles instead). Use Heading 1 for chapter titles, Heading 2 for sections. Body text as "Normal" style. This is the most critical step — garbage in, garbage out.

Word stylesFind & Replace cleanup
2
Convert to EPUB 3

Use Calibre (free), Vellum, or Sigil to convert .docx → EPUB 3. EPUB 3 is the current standard accepted by Apple Books, Kobo, Barnes & Noble, and IngramSpark digital. Kindle requires a separate step (below). Validate EPUB with EPUBCheck (free tool) — must pass with zero errors.

Calibre (free)Vellum (Mac)SigilEPUBCheck
3
Kindle Format (KDP)

Amazon KDP accepts EPUB 3 directly now (preferred) or you can use Kindle Previewer to convert and validate. Do NOT submit old MOBI format — Amazon deprecated it. Use KDP Previewer app to see exactly how it renders on all Kindle devices before submitting.

Amazon KDPKindle PreviewerEPUB 3 (submit directly)
4
eBook Cover

eBook cover is a single JPEG or PNG file. Minimum 2,560px on longest side. Aspect ratio: 1.6:1 (height:width) — standard is 1,600×2,560px. RGB color (not CMYK). 72 DPI is fine (screen only). High contrast text — must be legible as a thumbnail.

1600×2560px minRGB JPEG/PNGCanva / Photoshop
5
Metadata & Distribution

Set: Title, Subtitle, Author name (exactly as on cover), contributors, description (up to 4,000 chars on KDP — use HTML formatting), keywords (7 on KDP), BISAC categories (2), DRM preference, price by territory. For IngramSpark digital: upload EPUB + cover, set agency pricing.

BISAC codesKDP metadataIngramSpark digital
6
Free Digital Copy for Lead Gen (Apexum use)

For giving the eBook free to Apexum prospects: export a watermarked PDF version (PDF is simpler for free distribution than EPUB). Include a branded cover page and back page with Apexum CTA. Distribute via a landing page download link, email delivery, or BookFunnel (easiest tool for free book delivery).

BookFunnelWatermarked PDFLanding page linkEmail delivery
MANUSCRIPT → M4B AUDIOBOOK
ACX / Findaway Voices ready · M4B chapter format
PRODUCTION PIPELINE
1
Prepare the Reading Script

Create a clean reading script from the manuscript. Remove or adapt visual elements (tables, footnotes, image captions — read them aloud or skip with a note). Add pronunciation guides for any unusual names. Mark pauses with [PAUSE] tags. This is your narrator's working document.

Word doc reading scriptPronunciation guide
2
Recording Setup

ACX/Audible requires: 192kbps MP3 or WAV, Mono, -23dB RMS loudness, -3dB peak max, -60dB noise floor. Record each chapter as a separate file. Room treatment is critical — use a closet, hang blankets, or use a portable vocal booth. Condenser microphone minimum (Audio-Technica AT2020 or better).

ACX specsAudacity (free)Adobe Audition-23dB RMS
3
Audio Editing & Mastering

Edit each chapter: remove mouth noise, long silences, retakes. Apply: noise reduction, EQ (cut muddiness around 200-300Hz, boost clarity at 3-5kHz), light compression, final limiter. Normalize to -23dB LUFS (use Auphonic online — free, automated mastering to ACX specs).

AudacityAuphonic (auto-mastering)Adobe AuditioniZotope RX
4
Build the M4B File

M4B is AAC audio with chapter markers — the standard audiobook format for Apple Books, Findaway, and personal use. Steps: (1) Convert all chapter WAVs to M4A using ffmpeg or Fre:ac. (2) Merge into single M4B using Audiobook Builder (Mac), mp4box, or Chapter and Verse app. (3) Add chapter markers, cover art (1400×1400px min JPG), title/author metadata.

ffmpeg (free CLI)Audiobook Builder (Mac)Fre:ac (free)mp4box
5
QC Pass

Listen to the first and last 5 minutes of each chapter. Check: no clipping, consistent volume, no background hum, chapter markers correct, cover art embedded, metadata displays correctly in Apple Books / VLC. Run ACX Check plugin in Audacity to verify specs before submission.

ACX Check (Audacity plugin)Apple Books previewVLC
6
Distribution

ACX (Audible/Amazon exclusive or non-exclusive). Findaway Voices (wide distribution: Apple Books, Kobo, libraries — recommended for non-exclusive). Authors Republic (alternative wide). For Apexum free copy: distribute the M4B file directly via BookFunnel or as a downloadable link — M4B plays natively in iPhone Podcasts app and Apple Books.

ACX (Audible)Findaway VoicesBookFunnelApple Books

LOCKED TERMINOLOGY (enforced in all output)
Black Cherokee (cap) — UMBRELLA TERM, any era. Any person of Cherokee Nation membership or community who also has Black American lineage.
PRE-CIVIL WAR: "Cherokee slaves" / "enslaved people in the Cherokee Nation" for those in bondage. "Free Black Cherokees" / "free Blacks in the Cherokee Nation" for those not enslaved. Never call free Blacks slaves. Both groups are still Black Cherokees.
POST-TREATY OF 1866: "Cherokee Freedmen" = the historical generation freed from slavery by the Treaty of 1866. Used in research to mark social-political placement. After 1866 all received Cherokee citizenship and remained Black Cherokees.
LIVING DESCENDANTS: "Cherokee Freedmen descendants" or "descendants of Cherokee Freedmen." Inherited citizenship via the Dawes Freedmen line. There are NO living Cherokee Freedmen today.
DAWES ROLLS: Many enrollees were intermarried and had not been slaves. Their Cherokee blood was not recognized. Most descendants carry Cherokee AND European blood from slavery in the US, Cherokee Nation, or both. The assumption they lack Cherokee blood is a documented historical misunderstanding — address it when relevant.
"by blood" — in quotes, lowercase, first use per chapter  ·  No m-dashes in prose
